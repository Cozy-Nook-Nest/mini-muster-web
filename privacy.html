Mini Muster Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026 · Published by Cozy Nook Nest

In short: Mini Muster doesn't collect, send or sell any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your own device. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the app stores

Your miniatures, counts, notes, campaigns, encounters, settings and any photos you add are saved only on the device you're using:

  • iPhone and Android app: in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Windows app and web version: in your browser's storage on that computer.

We (Cozy Nook Nest) can't see, access or recover this information. It isn't uploaded anywhere, and it doesn't sync between devices.

Camera and photos

The app uses your camera or photo library only when you tap Take photo or Choose photo to add a picture of a miniature. The photo is resized and saved on your device with that miniature. It is never uploaded.

Things you paste in

If you paste a monster list or stat block into the app, it's read on your device to fill in the fields. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash reporting or third-party tracking code.

The website

The web version and this page are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to run and protect its service. We don't receive or use those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Deleting a miniature or photo in the app removes it from your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing this site's data in your browser, removes everything.

Children

Mini Muster doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
